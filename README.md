# WasaaLifestyle

A trust-centered household service marketplace for Nairobi. WasaaLifestyle helps
households find reliable service providers (such as plumbers and electricians)
when they have no personal referral to rely on. Its core contribution is a
**trust scoring model** that ranks providers by a computed measure of reliability
rather than a simple star-rating average.

## Overview

Existing platforms rank providers by a single star average, which is a weak and
easily manipulated signal of trust. WasaaLifestyle instead combines multiple
signals, verification status, job completion rate, complaint history, and
ratings, into a single, transparent trust score. Providers earn trust through a
layered verification model (identity, credential, and an earned track-record
tier) and through confirmed jobs over time.
